## 🌿 Professional Summary

Compassionate retreat facilitator, internationally exhibited Amazonian visionary artist, educator, translator, and Amazon expedition guide with more than 20 years of experience supporting international guests throughout Peru.

Educated in the artistic and philosophical tradition of the **Usko-Ayar Amazonian School of Painting** under the mentorship of **Pablo César Amaringo Shuña**. The school's philosophy emphasizes artistic excellence, integrity, respect for nature, cultural preservation, personal growth, and service to others — principles that continue to guide every aspect of David's retreat facilitation, teaching, and cross-cultural communication.

He draws on extensive personal experience as an *ayahuasquero* to provide compassionate participant support, foster meaningful communication between international guests and traditional healers, and help create safe, respectful, and transformative retreat experiences.
