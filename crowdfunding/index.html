Help Bring His Art and Teaching to the World

David’s family is currently facing financial hardship. GitHub Pages’ own terms of service explicitly permit “donation buttons and crowdfunding links” — this page is that: a place to support him directly, compliantly, while his art and teaching find a wider audience.

Coming soon

Real donation options — conventional and crypto — are being set up now. Crypto readiness matters specifically because David lives in Peru, where Ayahuasca work often runs into payment-processor stigma that crypto donations can sidestep.

A longer-range vision

Down the road, there’s an idea worth exploring: tokenizing David’s artwork as a real-world-asset attestation, with a future crowdsale or NFT mechanism to support him directly. That’s a genuine ambition, not a promise — it needs real legal and security review before anything is built, and isn’t part of this site yet.

This page does not process any transaction itself — it exists purely to showcase compliant donation-readiness. See the site’s source on GitHub.
