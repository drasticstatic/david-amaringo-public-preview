Ways to Give

Real donation options — conventional and crypto — are being set up now. Crypto matters specifically because David lives in Peru, where conventional payment processors often hesitate around Ayahuasca-related work. Crypto donations offer a transparent, legitimate channel for that same generosity to reach him directly — an act of love the law already recognizes as valid.

Transparency & Compliance

This page does not process any transaction itself — it exists purely to showcase compliant donation-readiness. Nothing on this site is for sale; every option here is donation-only, which keeps it squarely within what GitHub Pages’ Acceptable Use Policy permits. See the site’s source on GitHub.
