How This Could Work

  1. 1Connect a wallet — no different from any other web3 collector experience
  2. 2Choose an edition of one of David's pieces, each tied to a real, numbered print or a soulbound proof of patronage
  3. 3Funds go directly toward David and his family, transparently, on-chain
  4. 4Receive a collectible proof of patronage — not a speculative asset, a record of having supported real work
  5. 5Future editions and community mints are visible to everyone, the way the demo feed above illustrates

A Longer-Range Vision

Down the road, there’s an idea worth exploring: tokenizing David’s artwork as a real-world-asset (RWA) attestation, with a future crowdsale or NFT mechanism to support him directly and compliantly. That’s a genuine ambition, not a promise — it needs real legal and security review before anything is built, and isn’t part of this site’s live functionality yet.
