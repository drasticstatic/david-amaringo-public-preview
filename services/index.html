What David Offers

Services

A compassionate retreat facilitator, internationally exhibited Amazonian visionary artist, educator, translator, and Amazon expedition guide with more than 20 years of experience supporting international guests throughout Peru — trained since age 14 by his uncle, Pablo César Amaringo Shuña, in the artistic and philosophical tradition of the Usko-Ayar Amazonian School of Painting. He has served as Retreat Operations Coordinator with the Holy Earth Foundation since 2023, worked fifteen years alongside Maestro Víctor Nieto, and guided Amazon expeditions from 2005 to 2015.

Retreat Facilitation & Ceremony Co-Facilitation

Bilingual liaison and co-facilitator for Ayahuasca retreats across Peru — coordinating with traditional healers, retreat centers, and international guests, and providing steady, compassionate presence before, during, and after ceremony.

Visionary Art Instruction & Workshops

Teaching Neo-Amazonian visionary painting at the Usko-Ayar Amazonian School of Painting — mentoring students in composition, symbolism, color theory, and the deeper philosophy behind the craft.

Translation & Cultural Liaison

English–Spanish translation during ceremonies, consultations, and workshops — fostering real cross-cultural understanding between international guests and Amazonian healers.

Amazon Expedition Guiding

Guiding visitors through the Ucayali, Marañón, Yarapa, and Yanayacu river systems — Amazonian ecology, wildlife, medicinal plants, and Indigenous culture, from a decade guiding at Pink River Amazon Dolphin Lodge.

Commissioned Artwork

Original Amazonian visionary paintings, commissioned directly — internationally exhibited work, including at the Museum of Children's Art in Oslo, Norway.
